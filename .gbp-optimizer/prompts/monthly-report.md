# Prompt: Monthly GBP Report

Create a client-friendly monthly GBP/local SEO report from `.gbp-optimizer/tasks.md`, `.gbp-optimizer/cycles/`, `.gbp-optimizer/logs/`, and evidence.

Include:

- work completed
- ranking/conversion/review signals observed
- key issues found
- next priorities
- approvals needed
- client actions needed

Keep it factual and avoid guaranteeing ranking outcomes.
