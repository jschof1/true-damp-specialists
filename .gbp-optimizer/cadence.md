# GBP Optimisation Cadence

## Weekly

- Reply to new reviews.
- Draft/publish one GBP post.
- Upload fresh real photos.
- Capture competitor snapshot.
- Update `tasks.md`.

## Monthly

- Audit GBP fields, categories, services, products, photos, Q&A, and posts.
- Check NAP consistency and citation issues.
- Review Search Console and GBP insights where available.
- Run a review request campaign.
- Improve one service/location page.

## Quarterly

- Full competitor gap analysis.
- Citation/backlink campaign.
- Schema and technical local SEO check.
- Reassess service/location expansion.
- Review lead quality and conversion tracking.
