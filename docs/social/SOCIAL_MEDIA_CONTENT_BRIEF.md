# Social media content batch brief

## Goal

Create a substantial, reusable bank of social content for **True Damp Specialists** that can be posted across Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile, with light adaptation per platform where useful.

The content should make the company feel knowledgeable, independent and trustworthy, while turning common damp-related questions into useful posts that can generate enquiries.

## Deliverables

Create **30 finished, copy-ready social posts**.

For each post include:

1. **Post title / internal label**
2. **Hook**
3. **Finished caption**
4. **Clear CTA**
5. **Suggested platform(s)**
6. **Visual concept** for an image, carousel or simple graphic
7. **Alt text**
8. **A small, relevant hashtag set** where appropriate

Also provide:

- **10 short Reel / video hooks** that could be filmed quickly on a phone
- **5 carousel concepts** with slide-by-slide copy
- A simple suggested **4-week posting sequence** using the strongest pieces from the batch

Do **not** publish or schedule anything as part of this PR. This is a content-production task only.

## Content mix

Aim for a useful spread rather than 30 versions of the same sales post.

### 1. Education and diagnosis
Examples:
- why "rising damp" is often misdiagnosed
- condensation vs penetrating damp vs genuine rising damp
- what moisture meter readings do and do not prove
- why external ground levels, drainage, gutters or surface water can matter
- what salts, staining, mould or damaged plaster can indicate

### 2. Homeowner warning signs
Useful, non-alarmist posts explaining what people should look for and when it is sensible to get an investigation.

### 3. Myths and misconceptions
Challenge common damp-industry assumptions without attacking competitors or making claims we cannot substantiate.

### 4. Process and expertise
Explain how a proper investigation works, what evidence is considered, and why diagnosis should come before treatment.

### 5. Heritage and traditional buildings
Use the site's existing positioning around heritage and listed buildings. Explain vapour-permeable construction, inappropriate modern interventions, and conservation-minded diagnosis only where supported by existing site content.

### 6. Trust, proof and lead generation
Use real case studies, testimonials, accreditations, locations and proof already present in the repository/site. Turn them into social content without exaggeration.

## Brand / tone

Write in plain UK English.

The voice should be:

- expert but easy to understand
- calm and evidence-led
- helpful rather than alarmist
- confident without sounding salesy
- specific rather than generic
- written for homeowners, landlords, buyers and property professionals

Avoid cheesy marketing language, scare tactics, excessive emojis, generic AI phrasing and repetitive "Did you know?" openings.

## Geographic positioning

Use the current positioning from the repository:

- national coverage
- core focus on London, the Home Counties and the Midlands
- specialist projects, including heritage and listed buildings, can be undertaken more widely across the UK

Do not imply a physical office/address that is no longer used.

## Accuracy rules

Before writing, inspect the current site content and data files so posts reflect the real business.

In particular, use existing repository content for:

- services
- locations / coverage
- case studies
- testimonials
- accreditations
- phone/contact details
- guarantees or commercial claims

### Never invent

- customer reviews
- completed jobs
- before/after results
- certifications
- years of experience
- guarantees
- prices
- response times
- statistics
- locations served
- technical findings from jobs that are not documented

If an image or case-study detail is not verifiable from the repo, write a **visual brief** instead of pretending the asset exists.

## Conversion approach

Not every post should ask for a quote.

Rotate CTAs such as:

- save this for later
- send us a photo / question
- read the relevant guide
- book an independent damp investigation
- get in touch before paying for treatment
- share with someone buying or renovating an older property

Keep the CTA proportion sensible so the feed feels genuinely useful.

## Output files

Create:

- `docs/social/social-post-bank.md` for the 30 finished posts
- `docs/social/short-form-video-hooks.md` for Reel/video hooks
- `docs/social/carousel-concepts.md` for carousel slide copy

## Quality bar

The finished batch should feel like it came from someone who has actually read the True Damp Specialists website and understands the damp/diagnostics positioning.

Before finishing:

- check every factual claim against the repository
- remove duplicated angles
- make hooks meaningfully different
- ensure the captions are ready to paste, not just outlines
- ensure visuals are practical to create from existing site assets or simple branded graphics
